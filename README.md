# Introduction to Neuroinformatics

This repository contains my coursework for Introduction to Neuroinformatics at the University of Zurich. I’m interested in how individual neurons respond to input and what we can learn by looking at the activity of many neurons together.

## Topics I’m exploring

* **Single neurons:** How inputs arriving at different times or at different points on a neuron combine to change its membrane potential.
* **Neural populations:** How principal component analysis (PCA) helps reveal patterns in the activity of many neurons.
* **Neural representations:** How representational similarity analysis (RSA) compares patterns of activity across stimuli or conditions.
* **Synaptic plasticity:** How the timing of spikes can strengthen or weaken a connection between two neurons.

I update this repository as I work through the course.
